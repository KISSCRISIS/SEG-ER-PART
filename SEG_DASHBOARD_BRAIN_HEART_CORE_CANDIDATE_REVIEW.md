## Differences from the Approved Reference

The approved Dashboard core is a **contained professional-growth module**, not a standalone promotional banner. Its cyan brain rises above a compact red heart, with the heart projected from a cyan floor emitter. Five dark orbital course bubbles and a centered professional-growth heading establish its educational hierarchy. [1]

The candidate instead uses a **wide side-by-side brain/heart hero**. Its large embedded text—“Emergency Intelligence Core”—and three benefit chips create a second, incompatible information hierarchy. The ECG crosses the subject horizontally, while the reference reserves its ECG cue for the broader Dashboard header area. The large cyan-to-magenta outer border would also introduce a card-within-card effect that the reference does not contain. [1] [2]

## Required Corrections Before Production Consideration

The production candidate must be delivered as a **clean, isolated, composable asset** rather than as a rendered marketing board. It must preserve the reference’s vertical brain-over-heart arrangement, controlled cyan holographic base rings, dark transparent or clean navy surroundings, and restrained cyan/red grade. It must contain **no embedded wording, UI chips, panel border, promotional frame, or Login-labelled composition**.

The asset must also be visually checked against the final Dashboard core at its actual in-layout size. This review does not authorize any generated replacement, generic substitute, or use of the current candidate until a separate explicit approval is received.
