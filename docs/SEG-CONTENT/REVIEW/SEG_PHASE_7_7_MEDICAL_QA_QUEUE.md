## Review separation

Clinical review, rights review, track/taxonomy review, and implementation authorization remain separate streams. A structurally traceable extraction cannot be treated as medical approval or learner-facing content.

## Required evidence before QA closure

Each candidate still requires an attributable owner/publisher record, governed source version, precise content locator, explicit professional track and taxonomy, rights decision, clinical reviewer identity and decision, unresolved-issue disposition, and append-only audit evidence.
