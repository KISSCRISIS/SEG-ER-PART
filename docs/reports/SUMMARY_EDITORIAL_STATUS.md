# Summary editorial status

Three original high-yield summary samples were added to the unified guide for editorial calibration. They are intentionally concise, page-linked, and marked `draft` / `clinicalReview: pending`. They do not replace the source books or constitute an official Jordanian Board syllabus.

Before expanding the same template to the remaining units, a clinical reviewer should confirm the source locators, currency of recommendations, local protocols, and wording of every safety-critical statement. Medication doses are excluded from these samples until a current protocol is provided and reviewed.
