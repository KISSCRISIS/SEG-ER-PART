# SEG Unified Emergency Medicine Review Guide

This is an original, high-yield study guide derived from the selected Tintinalli and Rosen references. It is not a replacement or chapter-by-chapter reproduction. The source PDFs remain available for detailed reading.

## Required chapter template

Each chapter has: learning objectives, initial approach, diagnostic framework, management priorities, red flags, decision points, exam pearls, exam traps, Arabic plain-language explanation, source locators, review status, and linked SEG questions. Doses and time-sensitive recommendations require a current authoritative source and clinical review before publication.

## Coverage model

The guide targets approximately 400 pages after editorial condensation. Page count is a planning target, not a completeness claim. Coverage is measured against a versioned topic inventory; when an official Jordanian Board blueprint is unavailable, the dashboard must show “not measurable against official weights” rather than invent a percentage.

## Six plan mappings

Topics are tagged for Residency Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, Year 4, Jordanian Board Part 1, and Jordanian Board Part 2. Part 1 emphasizes basic principles and clinical science. Part 2 separates advanced clinical science, clinical competence, and OSCE preparation. These tags are study-routing metadata and are not an official Board blueprint.

## Current state

Rosh, Tintinalli Manual 8e, and Rosen & Barkin 5-Minute have page-linked intake manifests. The unified summaries, clinical review, conflict resolution, and final pagination remain pending. Every generated chapter must remain `under-review` until a named reviewer signs its source, currency, and clinical accuracy.

The editorial work queue is stored beside the guide as `editorial-queue.json`. Priority 1 covers high-risk emergency presentations, priority 2 covers core specialty and general emergency topics, and priority 3 covers supplementary reference topics. Queue status is not a coverage percentage.
