# Professional Growth Core

The My Home Professional Growth Core is a private-pilot competency dashboard for
emergency medicine residents. It is not a certification engine and does not make
clinical credentialing decisions.

## Framework Sources

- International Federation for Emergency Medicine: GME Emergency Medicine
  Curriculum 2025 Recommendations.
- ACGME: Emergency Medicine Milestones 2.0 and core competency framing.
- Royal College of Emergency Medicine: Emergency Medicine Curriculum 2021 with
  the 2025 update.

## Local SEG Domains

- Resuscitation: shock, cardiac arrest, airway, sepsis.
- Undifferentiated Care: acuity, triage, chief-complaint based reasoning.
- Procedures: airway, lines, sedation, POCUS, practical skills.
- Flow & Systems: handover, safety, quality improvement, crowding.
- Communication: team leadership, patients, families, ethics.
- Scholarship: evidence appraisal, reflection, teaching, personal notes.

## Scoring Status

Current percentages are pilot placeholders until a weighted scoring service joins
reading progress, Qbank performance, issue reports, journal review notes, and
supervised procedure records.
