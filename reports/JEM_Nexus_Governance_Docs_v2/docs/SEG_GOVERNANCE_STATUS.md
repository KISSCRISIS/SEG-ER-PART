### Entry Requirement

At least one source must achieve:

```text
READY_FOR_NORMALIZATION
```

after completion of:

- Source identity verification
- Provenance validation
- Rights assessment
- Medical review approval
- Required technical, visual, and accessibility reviews where applicable
- Explicit source-level governance approval

Artifact recovery, evidence-framework completion, or internal possession alone does not satisfy this entry requirement. Until the requirement is met, no normalization, database import, Prisma integration, Zod contract, SQL migration, activation, publication, or learner exposure is authorized.
