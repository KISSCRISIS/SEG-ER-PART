## 1. Purpose

This plan converts the completed `SOURCE_GOVERNANCE_GAP_MATRIX` into an execution-oriented workflow for acquiring, verifying, and packaging the evidence required to close the SEG Source Registry governance gaps. It is an evidence-management plan, not a content-processing or implementation plan.

The current source-registry decision remains `BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE`. The recovered files, hashes, structural counts, readability observations, and representation relationships are preserved as evidence, but they do not establish canonical authority, rights, clinical correctness, approval, professional-track placement, or readiness for normalization.[1] [2] [3]

> **Control principle:** `Source → Version → Governance Record → Clinical Review → Approval → Activation`. This plan covers evidence acquisition, evidence verification, reviewer assignment, and preparation of decision packages only. It does not perform or authorize the later implementation stages.

## 3. Execution states and priorities

The plan uses the statuses already established by the Source Closure Tracker. `OPEN` means that the required evidence is absent or unverified. `PARTIALLY_EVIDENCED` means that supporting files, hashes, structural observations, or recovery evidence exist, but the governance gate remains open. `NOT_AVAILABLE` means that no sufficient source-specific artifact or evidence is currently present. No current source gap is treated as closed.[3]

| Priority | Execution meaning | Closure implication |
|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Direct source-governance blocker | Canonical identity, original provenance, rights scope, required medical-review evidence, required restoration, or required inventory must be addressed before safe source-derived progression. |
| **P1** | Source-specific blocker | Track, locator, completeness, representation, and audit decisions must be resolved for the named source or representation. |
| **P2** | Pre-publication quality gate | Accessibility, display, readability, alt-text, and representation-quality checks must be completed before any learner-facing release, but they do not replace P0/P1 governance evidence. |

The P0 critical path is: **authoritative identity or source recovery → provenance linkage → rights evidence → reviewer assignment and clinical-review package → explicit track decision → source-specific audit closure**. P1 and P2 work may be prepared in parallel only where the prerequisite evidence is already available; no downstream activity may be represented as approval.

## 4. Common closure workflow

### 4.1 Evidence preservation and intake freeze

The evidence owner preserves every original and recovered representation in its existing location, records the immutable path, file type, size, SHA-256, source ID or representation ID when already supplied, and records whether the artifact is original, extracted, recovered, or a derivative representation. No new content record is created.

### 4.2 Identity and source-envelope acquisition

The source owner or authorized custodian supplies the canonical title, publisher or owner, author/editor where available, edition/version, publication date, and original locator. Identity evidence must be authoritative or independently verifiable and must be linked to the exact artifact or governed collection. Filename, topic similarity, embedded title signal, creator metadata, and publisher name alone are insufficient.

### 4.3 Provenance verification

The provenance reviewer documents the chain **original source → extraction event → recovery/restoration event → existing registry evidence**, including event context, timestamps where available, paths or archive members, input/output hashes, and any transformation or restoration boundary. For a representation that has not been verified against the original, the missing relationship remains explicitly unresolved.

### 4.4 Rights and privacy evidence

The rights reviewer obtains a source-specific rights decision or permission package that states the rights holder, permitted SEG use, display scope, derivative-use scope, restrictions, evidence locator, and review date where applicable. For clinical images, privacy, consent, de-identification, and any patient or model-release requirement are separate evidence requirements. A rights status is not inferred from public availability, file possession, or a source label.

### 4.5 Clinical review preparation

The medical-governance owner assigns a reviewer role only when the assignment can be made from supplied governance authority; this plan does not invent reviewer identities. The review package must specify the clinical domain, exact source/version and locators, coverage, conflicts, unresolved issues, decision, date, and approval evidence requirements. Visual readability, structural counts, or extracted medication entities are not clinical approval.

### 4.6 Track, locator, and audit closure

The curriculum owner and clinical reviewer record an explicit decision among the permitted SEG pathway classifications: Internship, Emergency Medicine Resident, Shared Core, or Advanced Reference, while preserving the seven governed project tracks and never inferring level from filenames or difficulty labels. The provenance/governance owner records exact locators and append-only events for intake, hashing, recovery, rights, clinical review, track decision, and later gate decisions.

### 4.7 Source-by-source closure decision

A source may be considered evidence-closure ready only when the required identity, provenance, rights, clinical-review, track, and audit evidence is independently present, internally consistent, linked to the relevant artifact SHA or collection, and reviewed by the appropriate governance role. This plan does not grant that state. Any conflicting, missing, or non-attributable evidence remains `BLOCKED` or `UNRESOLVED`.

## 6. Execution sequencing and handoff controls

### Wave 0 — Common evidence-control setup

The governance owner establishes the evidence request template, source-family identifiers, immutable intake locations, evidence naming convention, SHA recording convention, reviewer-role fields, unresolved-issue register, and append-only event requirements. This is documentation and evidence organization only; it is not a schema or database design.

### Wave 1 — Critical source recovery and inventory

The first operational wave addresses the sources with missing authoritative artifacts or inventories: **Clinical Images**, **Pretest**, **OSCE**, **1400 Questions**, and the Drug HTML source envelope. Clinical Images requires an artifact inventory before any image-level review. Pretest requires authoritative restoration or verification before clinical review. OSCE requires source-owner context for its recovered container and PNG members. 1400 Questions requires independent treatment of the study-guide and Q&A representations. Drug HTML requires authoritative source-envelope recovery before its medication content can be reviewed as source-derived material.

### Wave 2 — Bibliographic and rights closure

After source identity or recovery evidence is available, the rights reviewer evaluates the intended use for each family and representation. HIPPO HY, HIPPO QBank, FRCEM, and Oxford require source-specific bibliographic and rights evidence rather than inference from a product name, local metadata, filename, or publisher signal. Any restriction or uncertainty remains explicitly recorded.

### Wave 3 — Reviewer assignment and decision-package preparation

The governance owner prepares separate clinical, rights, track/taxonomy, provenance, and accessibility review packets. Medical reviewers receive exact source versions and locators; rights reviewers receive the relevant license or permission evidence; curriculum owners receive verified source scope rather than filenames or assumed exam level. A reviewer assignment is not a clinical approval.

### Wave 4 — Track, locator, and audit closure

For each source, the curriculum owner and clinical reviewer record the explicit professional-track decision. The provenance/governance owner records source-versioned locators and missing append-only events. Representation relationships remain separate unless an authorized, evidence-supported comparison establishes a relationship; even then, preservation of original files remains mandatory.

### Wave 5 — Evidence-closure gate review

A later gate review checks each source family against the criteria in Section 5 and classifies it as `PASS`, `BLOCKED`, or `UNRESOLVED` based only on supplied and independently attributable evidence. The gate review must preserve negative, conflicting, or incomplete evidence. It must not create a content record, mapping, import payload, database object, or activation decision.

## 7. Stop conditions

Execution stops for the affected source when source identity cannot be authenticated, the original source is unavailable where required, the artifact cannot be matched to its claimed source, rights scope is missing or contradictory, the clinical reviewer cannot be assigned or the review evidence is incomplete, the track conflicts, the representation relationship is unresolved, or the required inventory/locator/audit evidence is absent.

The following conditions do not justify progression: a matching SHA alone, a readable file, a structural entity count, a filename, a publisher signal embedded in content, a local author field, a visual-readability note, a README description, or an apparent exam level. Each may remain useful supporting evidence, but none closes a governance gate independently.[1] [2] [3]
