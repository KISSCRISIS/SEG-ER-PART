## 1. Completion instructions

Complete this template through the owner-governed SEG review pathway by an authorized, attributable Assessment Quality Reviewer or an owner-executed review authority with recorded qualification and conflict controls. No mandatory external reviewer or external approval dependency is assumed solely because the asset is internal. Do not infer, correct, rewrite, reorder, or supplement questions, answers, explanations, numbering, or OCR text. Record only findings supported by the exact retained artifact, the authoritative source/version evidence, the immutable comparison, and cited review references. If evidence is unavailable, record `NOT_SUPPLIED`, `NOT_AVAILABLE`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

This template covers Assessment Quality review only. Emergency Medicine clinical review, Technical/OCR review, rights review, provenance review, track/taxonomy review, and source approval remain separate governance streams. A completed assessment review does not authorize normalization, import, activation, publication, or learner exposure.
