## 1. Purpose and control boundary

This tracker operationalizes the approved full-content recovery plan for the seven source families required by SEG. It records the current state of retained artifacts, the evidence already available, the evidence still missing, ownership roles for the next recovery action, blocking conditions, and the next permitted action. It is a governance-management artifact only.

No row in this tracker authorizes normalization, medical-content correction, source substitution, mapping, schema creation, SQL, database modification, import, seed, activation, publication, or learner exposure. A cataloged or recovered artifact remains an evidence item and does not become a production content record by virtue of being listed here.[1]

## 2. Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `AVAILABLE — REPRESENTATION EVIDENCED` | A retained representation or recovery artifact is present and has identifiable integrity or registry evidence, but the complete source-governance envelope is not necessarily closed. |
| `PARTIALLY_RECOVERED` | Some artifacts or representations are present, while the authoritative source, completeness, rights, or lineage evidence remains open. |
| `NOT AVAILABLE` | The source-specific artifact or required evidence package is not present in the reviewed set. |
| `PENDING_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE` | Ownership, license, authoritative identity, or another external fact has not been established by attributable evidence. |
| `NOT_STARTED` | No attributable review or closure event is recorded for the stated domain. |
| `BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE` | A required governance gate remains open; downstream processing is not authorized. |
| `INTERNAL_REFERENCE_ONLY` | Controlled preservation and inspection are permitted; educational, production, publication, and learner-facing use are not permitted. |

## 4. Cross-source recovery controls

The following controls apply to every row:

| Control | Tracker rule |
|---|---|
| Required-source preservation | All seven families remain `REQUIRED`. No source may be discarded, retired, silently replaced, renamed, merged, or substituted under this tracker. |
| Evidence status | Existing hashes, registry paths, structural observations, and recovery notes establish only the stated representation-level facts. They do not prove ownership, permission, completeness, clinical correctness, or approval. |
| Rights handling | Rights remain `PENDING_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE` until source-specific ownership and permitted-use evidence is attributable and documents educational use, display, attribution, redistribution, and publication limits. |
| Medical review | `NOT_STARTED` means no attributable clinical review or approval evidence is recorded. Reviewer roles are placeholders for assignment, not named approvals. |
| Provenance | Every future closure package must document `Original Source → Extraction Event → Recovery Artifact → SEG Registry`, with representation-specific hashes, dates, locators, and an attributable custodian. |
| Track separation | Internship, Resident, Shared Core, and Advanced classifications must be supported by explicit evidence. No filename, format, question count, or catalog signal closes a professional-track decision. |
| Recovery versus production | Evidence recovery may preserve and hash evidence in a controlled package; it does not create production content, mappings, or learner access. |

## 5. Recovery sequence reflected by this tracker

Recovery proceeds in controlled source-family order, while all seven families remain required:

1. **P0 continuity and source restoration:** Drug HTML, OSCE Atlas, and PreTest receive targeted identity, rights, provenance, and review evidence actions first. PreTest requires restoration or an independently verifiable equivalent of the authoritative source before OCR-related comparison can close.
2. **P1 representation and completeness closure:** HIPPO HY, HIPPO QBank, and FRCEM receive authoritative identity, rights, representation/completeness, locator, provenance, and reviewer evidence. FRCEM study-book and Q&A representations remain separately reviewable.
3. **Clinical Images package recovery:** Clinical Images requires an independent artifact recovery and per-file evidence inventory. OSCE PNGs must not be counted toward this source family.
4. **Governance review:** Evidence packages are submitted for identity, rights, medical-review, professional-track, and provenance decisions. This tracker records no approval.
5. **Downstream gates:** A source may be reconsidered for normalization only after its source-specific evidence is complete and the applicable governance owner approves the normalization review gate. Database integration remains a separate, later gate requiring approved content-contract, mapping, database-design, security, adapter/import-QA, and release decisions.[1]
