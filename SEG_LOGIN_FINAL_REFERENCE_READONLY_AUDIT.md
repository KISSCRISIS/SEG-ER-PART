## Required Reference Contract

The approved composition uses a centered, large official SEG brain-heart-ECG lockup and the exact tagline **“Emergency Medicine Learning Platform”**. It places a tall dark-glass Login card below that lockup, a large red intelligence-heart stage to the left, a blue brain-with-heart intelligence stage to the right, and a continuous red/blue ECG line across the scene. The card includes Email Address and Password controls, remembered-session control, recovery link, Login call to action, branded social choices, Register link, and two compact trust panels below.
