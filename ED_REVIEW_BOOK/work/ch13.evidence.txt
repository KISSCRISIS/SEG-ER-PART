Q: A 62-year-old man presents with acute onset of chest pain with radiation to the shoulders and nausea while walking his dog. A: Left main coronary artery
EXPLANATION: ST segment elevation in lead aVR > 1 mm or greater than the ST elevation seen in lead V1 is most concerning for occlusion or subocclusion of the left main coronary artery.
