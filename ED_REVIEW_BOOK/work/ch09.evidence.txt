Q: Which of the following symptoms is most consistent with systemic juvenile idiopathic arthritis?
A: Intermittent high fever
EXPLANATION: Systemic juvenile idiopathic arthritis (sJIA) is characterized by fever, arthritis, and rash occurring in children up to 16 years of age. All sexes are affected equally.
