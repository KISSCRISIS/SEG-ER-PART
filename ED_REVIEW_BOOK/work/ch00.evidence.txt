Q: A 5-year-old with status epilepticus. Two doses of lorazepam ineffective. What medication should be administered next?
A: Levetiracetam IV

Q: Procedural sedation on a 65-year-old. Repeated sternal rubbing required to elicit any response, transient hypopnea and airway obstruction managed with BVM and jaw thrust. HR and BP normal. What level of sedation does this represent?
A: Deep sedation
EXPLANATION: Deep sedation occurs when the patient requires repeated painful stimulation to cause any response. Spontaneous breathing and airway patency may be transiently compromised. ARABIC: تهدئة عميقة (deep sedation): استجابة فقط للمنبهات المؤلمة المتكررة مع احتمال اضطراب مؤقت في التنفس والمجرى الهوائي. لا يفقد الاستجابة الكاملة كما في التخدير العام.

Q: A 56-year-old woman with flu-like symptoms, several family members have similar symptoms, cluster indoors in cold weather. Lactate 3.4, VBG pH 7.22. Which additional laboratory test is essential?
A: Carboxyhemoglobin level
EXPLANATION: Family cluster indoors in cold weather = possible carbon monoxide poisoning. Elevated lactate from cellular hypoxia. CO poisoning can cause hypotension, falsely normal SpO2, and neurologic symptoms. Essential to test carboxyhemoglobin.
ARABIC: تسمم بأول أكسيد الكربون (CO poisoning): عائلة في مكان مغلق بجو بارد + حماض لبني. SpO2 يبدو طبيعيًا كاذبًا. الفحص الأساسي هو carboxyhemoglobin.

Q: A 20-year-old woman with tick bite noted after a hike in New York 48 hours ago. What further management is needed after removal?
A: Administer a single 200-mg dose of doxycycline to prevent Lyme disease
EXPLANATION: A single dose of doxycycline 200 mg can prevent Lyme disease if given within 72 hours of tick removal in endemic areas with deer tick identified and attached >36 hours. Amoxicillin is ineffective for prophylaxis.

Q: A 28-year-old man with epilepsy presents with status epilepticus. After 8 mg lorazepam IV, seizure continues. What is the appropriate next treatment?

EXPLANATION: Status epilepticus is a seizure lasting >5 minutes or intermittent without return to baseline. After adequate benzodiazepine dosing, IV antiepileptics include phenytoin, fosphenytoin, valproate, levetiracetam, propofol, or barbiturates. Valproate dosing for status: 20-30 mg/kg.

### [PEER-CS | PEER-key-advances-q-203]
Q: A 50-year-old woman after camping in Portland, Maine. Fever, chills, lymphadenopathy, fatigue, myalgias, headache, rash (targetoid). What is the best next step in risk stratification of this patient?
A: ECG
EXPLANATION: Lyme disease with erythema migrans. An ECG will allow risk stratification of Lyme carditis (mild: first-degree AV block with PR <300 ms; severe: PR ≥300 ms, second- or third-degree block). Symptomatic patients with severe findings require hospitalization and IV antibiotics.
ARABIC: داء لايم مع حمامى مهاجرة (erythema migrans): يجب عمل ECG لتقييم التهاب القلب اللايمي (Lyme carditis) — حصار AV من الدرجة الأولى أو الأعلى. الحالات الشديدة (PR ≥300 ms أو حصار من الدرجة الثانية/الثالثة) تتطلب إدخالًا ومضادات وريدية.
